What's in this unit?

Dividing numbers up to 4 digits by a 1-digit number, deciding what to do with a remainder in a word problem, finding a missing side of a rectangle, multi-step word problems, and number and shape patterns.

How we divide in class: we break the number into place-value chunks (partial quotients and the area model) before using the long-division shortcut. The "Show me how" helper follows those same steps, so you and your child will be speaking the same language.

Words to know

Dividend
The number being divided. In 84 ÷ 4, it's 84.
Divisor
The number you divide by. In 84 ÷ 4, it's 4.
Quotient
The answer to a division problem. 84 ÷ 4 = 21.
Remainder
The amount left over after making equal groups. It's always smaller than the divisor.
Partial quotients
Dividing in friendly chunks (like hundreds, then tens, then ones) and adding the chunks together.
